**What is your job here at THS?**

My official job title is Intervention Coordinator, but nobody calls me that. I help coordinate the Student Assistance Program (SAP), I monitor all ETEH’s, I work with kids in work programs, I deal with attendance, specifically truancy and chronic absenteeism, I’m an associate liaison to Butler Tech, which means I coordinate field trips to Butler Tech and help kids apply. I coordinate the PBIS (positive behavioral intervention and supports) committee (bulletin board with names), I also help students with helping find pathways to graduation. The most important part of my job is random duties assigned, which means I do random tasks to help kids.

**What’s your favorite part of the job?**

I like helping students find success.

**What’s your least favorite part?**

There’s enough negativity in the world. We don’t need to have more here.

**Where might people see you in the building?**

My office is next to the clinic, in the 500 hallway, but you can also see me occasionally in classes, or the counselor’s office.

**What’s your favorite school subject, and why?**

I love math. How many thousands of words can this article be? Because I love talking about math. I have my undergrad in Math & Statistics and a Master’s in Math Education. I just love doing math.

**If you could dispense a liquid from each of your five fingers, what would it be?**

Coffee, Water, Coke Zero, vanilla milkshake, and gasoline- wait replace milkshake with ink, because then I could use it like a pen.
