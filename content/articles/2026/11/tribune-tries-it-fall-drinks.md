Oct. 22, 2026

Oxford, OH—The leaves are changing. The jack o lanterns are staring. Our timbers are shivered. It’s time for Tribune to once again try Kofenya’s fall drinks. On the the menu this season are:

![](/media/tribune-tries-it-fall-drinks-image6.jpg)

The best tasting were Booberry and Witches Brew. The fact that Witches Brew scored that highly shocked Waxy who thinks Matcha usually tastes like freshly-mowed grass. Miriam thinks Matcha is good with stuff in it but alone, it’s disgusting. The worst tasting drink was by far the Jack o’ Lantern cold brew (think of pumpkin spice perfume). We had high expectations for this one, but they were smashed just like pumpkins on November 1.
