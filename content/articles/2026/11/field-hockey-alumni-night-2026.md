![](/media/field-hockey-alumni-night-2026-image1.jpg)The Oxford Brave Field Hockey team has had an eventful season this year and ended it with a blast with the first ever Annual Alumni Game! On October 7th the event mixed current team members with previous field hockey players to play the most epic games the seasons combined had to offer.

Not only was this event so fun but for the first time it reunited the Brave Field Hockey Family! Some Alumni were interviewed and we asked the experienced about their favorite memories playing and their advice for current players of the game.

Talawanda Field Hockey Alumni Dotti Thacker played all four years of high school and came back to the Alumni Night for her love of the game and to support the current team. Her favorite field hockey memory was going to state after a season undefeated. Her advice to players today is to never get frustrated, and never give up, mistakes happen.

Talawanda Field Hockey Alumni Payton played all four years of high school as well and came back to the Alumni Night because it is her favorite sport ever and she misses it. Her favorite field hockey memory was her senior all-star game. Her advice to players today is to be confident in what you do and don't let wins inflate your ego or losses bring you down.

The Brave Field Hockey community hopes this night to be the first of many and to see all these faces again next year at the next game!
