**2026 Halloween Costume Predictions**

Emily Luebbe · October 4th, 2026

**1. Dolly Parton**

This year we lost one of America’s biggest icons–Dolly Parton. So obviously, the best way to honor her is to pull out your big blonde wigs and cowboy boots to go ask strangers for candy.

![](/media/2026-halloween-costume-predictions-image4.jpg)

**2. Spider-Man**

This summer brought a lot of new trends to the internet but pretending to be dangling from the ceiling was not one that I expected. Nevertheless, Spider-Man will always be a staple Halloween costume.

![](/media/2026-halloween-costume-predictions-image3.jpg)

**3. Butter Squishy**

Nobody is quite sure who decided that 2026 was going to be the new 2016, but somehow it has held true. One of the biggest exhibits of this nostalgia-filled year is the resurgence of fidgets. Butter squishies are a great way to forget any work you were given and they give you the chance to truly be a kid again.

![](/media/2026-halloween-costume-predictions-image1.jpg)

**4. *Stranger Things***

The Halloween season is a great time to curl up with a creepy show. If you enjoy paper mache, handmaking a Vecna costume could be a great use of your time. Or if you’re on more of a time crunch, I would suggest raiding your parents’ closet for some good 90’s outfits.

![](/media/2026-halloween-costume-predictions-image5.jpg)

**5. Olivia Rodrigo**

With her newest album, *you seem pretty sad for a girl so in love*, the Olivia Rodrigo themed costumes will be off the charts this year.

![](/media/2026-halloween-costume-predictions-image7.jpg)

**6. *Toy Story***

*Toy Story 5* broke many global box office records, so it wouldn’t be surprising to see some of the newer characters showing up in Halloween shops. And, of course, the classic characters we know and love will make an appearance as well.

![](/media/2026-halloween-costume-predictions-image6.jpg)

**7. Obsession**

Probably most peoples’ favorite movie of 2026, Obsession broke many records and was very popular. The characters are pretty easy to represent in a costume, so there is no doubt teens all over the country will be recreating the look.

![](/media/2026-halloween-costume-predictions-image2.jpg)
