Fall Sports at Talawanda have been truly outstanding so far this year for every team. Despite some concerns from past seasons, our teams have really locked in and are really thriving. Soccer, Golf, Tennis, Volleyball, and Football are all performing on a superb level. The 2026 season will be remembered for years to come.

Soccer has been having a historic season, the best since 2018, currently with a record of 11-2-1.

Only losing to Monroe, the boys have been looking defensively in tune and have made a massive improvement in attacking and striking since the 2025 season. This season has been led by seniors Brayden Kent and Noel Albarran who have both scored many goals. The girls have also been having a decent season, currently with a record of 6-5-3. ![](/media/fall-sports-alert-image1.png)[![](/media/fall-sports-alert-image2.png)](https://www.talawandaathletics.org/boys-soccer/#)

Talawanda golf has been very promising this season as the boys varsity team is 7-2. The girls are also on fire as they have just recently won the OHSAA Division II Championship with a score of 357 which marks this as the first time in Talawanda history a golf team has advanced to state.

![](/media/fall-sports-alert-image4.png)

The Talawanda girl’s tennis team has been doing pretty well this season as well, holding a league record of 3-2 and an overall record of 7-6. The challenges will continue to come as they keep playing in the Southwestern Buckeye League.

![](/media/fall-sports-alert-image3.png)

Our volleyball team is also doing pretty well this season going 6-7 overall so far but 3-2 in their conference season, holding the third place title. Their most recent game against Franklin was a massive blowout going 3-0 against the Wildcats. More victories are sure to come for the girls.

![](/media/fall-sports-alert-image6.png)

Finally, to end the latest in Talawanda sports news, the Football team. With a history season last year many fans were a bit skeptical about this year considering how many incredibly talented players graduated and would not be returning for the 2026 season. But this year is living proof that 2025 was no fluke, Talawanda’s Football team is having another exciting season going 5-1, half of the regular season went undefeated and the one loss we did suffer against Ross was very close to the final whistle blow and some questionable referring decisions. This year’s team is arguably even more balanced this year compared to last and we can expect to go very far with the sheer amount of drive and talent this year’s squad possess.![](/media/fall-sports-alert-image5.png)
