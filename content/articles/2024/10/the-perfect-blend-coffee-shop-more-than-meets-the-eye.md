# The Perfect Blend Coffee Shop: More Than Meets the Eye

Ian Sullivan and Derek Manukian-Kazarian

Oct. 10, 2024

![](/media/the-perfect-blend-coffee-shop-more-than-meets-the-eye-image1.jpg)

The Perfect Blend Coffee Shop — a school based business operated by the special education students — is much more than just a coffee shop. The amazing drinks and fabulous customer service has led to fame among the student body, despite this, little know how the Perfect Blend Coffee Shop got its start and what happens after hours.

Jennifer George, the mastermind behind the Perfect Blend Coffee Shop and our interviewee, about the Perfect Blend’s beginning said, “In my fifth year teaching it was covid times, and the teacher who thought about starting this program applied for a grant from the Oxford Community Foundation and got \$2,000 to open a coffee shop. But during covid times, clearly I couldn’t serve coffee to everyone wearing a mask, so it took two years for me to finally be able to use the funds and start the coffee shop.” The Perfect Blend isn’t just a small coffee shop with humble beginnings, it’s also an amazing work experience for Georges’ students. “Class 23 is what my program is called. It is a Transition to Work program so we focus on life, leisure and employability skills. So every one of my students gets one shift in the coffee shop per week.”

![](/media/the-perfect-blend-coffee-shop-more-than-meets-the-eye-image2.jpg)

Student customers and workers at The Perfect Blend (Photo Credit: Lauren Dysert)

When hearing about the coffee shop you may be wondering where the money made from drinks goes. Well, it’s all put right back into the program. George said, “I do a lot of things with the funds, like rebuying what I need to run the coffee shop. So I need to make sure I buy all the things that we’ve used, like the hot chocolate and the cups and the straws and lids. I also use money to take field trips with my class and if my students need to buy lunch because we got to eat every Friday. But it all goes back into my program.”

To the eye of the student body the Perfect Blend Coffee Shop is just an ordinary coffee shop, but it is so much more. The stellar work experiences the students get is proof enough.
