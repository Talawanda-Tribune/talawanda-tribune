Imagine. You walk into the library-- not just any library, the Oxford Lane Public Library --- home to hundreds of books and movies stored inside. You smile. You breathe in the scent of fresh books. You scour the decade old shelves with books filled with fictional tales of kings, queens, forbidden romances… all the usual.

You suddenly feel bored. Not of the books or movies, but just really unmotivated. Have you ever felt like that? Excited at the idea of finding new books to read but not feeling in the mood? Or maybe you aren’t a big reader. Perhaps you prefer to do something more.. Like a club or activity.

Well, look no further, because the Oxford Lane Public Library (or OLPL) has got you covered teens! Not only is it home to thousands of books and movies, it is also home to fun activities for teens. But what exactly are these **upcoming events**, and would you find it interesting? Please come have a look.

**Poet-Tea**

Meets: Saturday, April 5th, 2025 (3:00 PM - 5:00 PM)

Age range: 14 and up (Teens, adults)

Located: Oxford Lane Public Library (Oxford Havighurst Meeting Room)

If you’re interested in poetry, you might enjoy Poet-Tea. Here is some information on the event according to the OLPL website (when you click on this event on the calendar:

**“It’s National Poetry Month! If poetry is your cup of tea, sign up for a poetry writing class/tea party with a published poet, artist and teacher with an MFA in poetry from MU, Hallie Fogarty. Refreshments and notebooks provided.”**

If any of this seems interesting to you, I recommend trying! (Plus, it’s National Poetry Month.)

**Play with the Experts: Super Smash Bros**

Meets: Friday, April 18th, 2025 (6:00 PM - 7:30 PM)

Age range: 12 to 18 (Teens, adults)

Located: Oxford Lane Public Library (Oxford Havighurst Meeting Room)

If you’re not into books as much, you may like this. Here’s some information about the event (as of now):

**“..play with Miami University’s own Varsity Esports Super Smash Bros. Ultimate team. All skill levels are welcome and controllers are provided. Just come and have fun!”**

If you’re interested in playing video games with others for fun, this just may be your cup of tea!

**The Learning Lab @Lane for Grades 6 - 8**

Meets: Thursdays (See [calendar](https://www.lanepl.org/events/month/2025/03?age_groups%5B3%5D=3&branches%5B82%5D=82) for more details)

Age range: Teens (Grades 6, 7, and 8)

Located: Oxford Lane Public Library (Oxford Havighurst Meeting Room)

This is basically tutoring. I realize this may not seem as entering, and I realize it is more focused on tweens than anything, but I felt like I should put this on here. Even if it isn’t for you-- do you have any siblings or friends who may need this or want extra help? Here is some information to help:

**“Does your child need extra help with math or reading/ELA? Please call (513) 523-7531 ext. 1218 to sign up for one free 30-minute tutoring session a week with tutors from Miami’s Learning Lab. Registration will open exactly one week prior to each session. (A parent or caregiver must stay in the library for the duration of their child’s tutoring session.)”**

If this is for you or someone else, I hope it helps. Remember: It is for grades 6-8 so it **cannot** be for highschool students. (As far as I’m aware.) Enjoy!
