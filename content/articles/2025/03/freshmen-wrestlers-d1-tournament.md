Harrison, OH--Ninth graders Mikaela Lema and Mili Malohn ended their first year as high school wrestlers with a lot to proud of and have given their coaches much to look forward to next season.

At sectionals on Feb. 21, 2025, Lema took first place in the 115 lb division after winning her first two matches. Malohn took fourth place in the 100 lb division. Both wrestlers earned spots at the OHSAA District Tournament.

Lema and Malohn each competed in two matches in their divisions on Mar. 2 at Districts at William Henry Harrison High School. Although neither athlete qualified for the State Tournament to be held on Mar. 7-9 at Ohio State, they have three more seasons of what is certain to be stellar wrestling ahead of them.

This will be the third year for an OHSAA-sanctioned girls’ wreslting tournament, and according to OHSAA, Olentangy Orange and Harrison “have emerged as the programs possessing the best combination of both quantity and quality as the sport continues to grow in status and caliber.”

Harrison competes in the Division I Southwest region with Talawanda and took first place as a team at Districts.
