# A Royal Edit: Tribune Co-Editors Win Prom Queen and King

Amanda Weatherwax· April 27, 2025

Oxford, OH—Talawanda High School’s prom, themed “A Night in Hollywood,” took place on Saturday, April 26, 2025, at Spooky Nook in Hamilton. Tribune co-editor Lauren Dysert was crowned Prom Queen, and Tribune co-editor Thomas Harper was named Prom King during the evening’s festivities.![](/media/a-royal-edit-tribune-co-editors-win-prom-queen-and-king-image2.png)

The event began at 6:30 PM, with students arriving in formal attire to enjoy an elegant evening full of glitz and glamour. The night’s theme, highlighted by red and gold décor, set the tone for a Hollywood-inspired celebration.

Dinner was served from 7:00 to 8:30 PM and featured a delicious menu of salad, seasonal vegetables, scalloped potatoes, chicken parmesan, and cheese ravioli. The dance began at 8:30 PM and lasted until 11:00 PM.

The evening was coordinated by Danielle Mann, whose efforts ensured that the event ran seamlessly from start to finish. The venue sparkled with red and gold accents, and the music kept the dance floor busy all night.

Overall, the 2025 prom at Spooky Nook was a memorable and successful event, bringing students together for an unforgettable celebration before the end of the school year.
